The most enduring lesson here is generously simple: if a person cannot tell what to do, look at the object before you look at the person.

## What stayed with me

Good design gives feedback, makes the next action visible, and has the grace to make mistakes recoverable. These are modest ambitions, but they transform how something feels to use.
