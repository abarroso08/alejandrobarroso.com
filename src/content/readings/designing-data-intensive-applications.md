This book makes distributed systems feel less like magic and more like a collection of honest compromises. Every chapter returns to the same useful question: what can go wrong, and what are we promising when it does?

## A note I’m keeping

Reliability is not a feature you bolt on at the end. It is a posture: expecting partial failure, naming trade-offs, and designing for the person who has to understand the system at 2 a.m.
