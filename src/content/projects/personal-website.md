This is an intentionally small website. I wanted a place that puts words and work before feeds, dashboards, and endless customization.

## The idea

It is built as a collection of pages rather than an app. Projects and reading notes begin as Markdown files, so publishing a thought is as close to writing a note as possible.

The goal is not to look busy. It is to leave enough room for the work to speak.
