I have started my university journey almost 5 years ago. In this 5 years almost no teacher has taught it as something important for our career.
Instead, I have been fighting with it since first I met it when coding some of my first websites. And the problem has always been the same. I always think I know how to use it until I find myself infront of a completly no sense nightmare of commits, pull requests, collisions...

Today I am not in that situation but I have realised I do not want to just have a basic understanding of it. I want to be a master, not to just run the six basic commands because I learned them by heart and they just work. I want to go ahead at full speed, revisit the whole git system from scratch and build my git and github workflow based on knowledge and not on spitting commands that I don't fully understand but just work.
