AISC Madrid needed a website that made it easier to understand who they are and how to get involved. The work focused on clarity, a warm identity, and content that volunteers could keep current.

## A good constraint

Keeping the stack uncomplicated meant more time could go toward the people using it. That is often the right trade-off for a community project.
